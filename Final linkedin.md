Among all the problems a growing product can have, the quietest one is this:
Everyone can explain their own part well, but no one can explain how it all fits together.

The product works. There are real users, real workflows, real engineers.
The problem isn’t that something has broken. The problem is that you’re starting to lose the ability to know what might break.

Very rarely is this about the quality of the work. Mostly, the product has just outgrown the team’s shared understanding of it.
