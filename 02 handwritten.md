 Before you build more:Define the product again.

Your product has been working for months. You have real users, real workflows, real engineers.And then, at some point, nothing is obviously broken—but you start wondering: If we keep building the way we are today, will this product actually take us where we want to go?

If the honest answer is “I’m not really sure,” it may be time to stop building for a moment and define the product again.
