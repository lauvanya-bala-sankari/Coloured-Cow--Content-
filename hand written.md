      congratulations. having started as a small idea into a working product that is running for months and having real users.your product worked and people are depending on it.thats real and huge!but for a product that has reached this stage may also experience many problems along the way to actually reach that visionary next stage of the product. some of them are components breaking, integrations giving away, shipping getting slower and much more.to be more precise.check if you have encounter any of the situations below ,
 1, having a half second flinch before approving a change that used to be routine.
 2,sitting in a meeting and realizing mid-sentence that you can actually explain what these two parts of your product do, but cant explain  why touching one quietly affects the other .
 3,its your best engineer saying 'let me check' about the system they built .
 4,Features that used to ship in days now take weeks — not because anyone got slower, but because every change has to be traced through parts of the system
 5,new people taking more time to become useful 
yeah, i know everything here is fixable on your own but one important and distinguishable question you may ask yourself is "will this system (as it is now) can carry platform where you wanted it to go?" if the answer is no, then you are probably experiencing "the problem of legibility". the classic sign is everybody can explain their own parts but no one could explain how all of this will fit together.what is lacking is the shared understanding of the product itself.
