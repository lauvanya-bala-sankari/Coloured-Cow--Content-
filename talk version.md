the product is like a child to a founder. like every parent , the founder wants the child to achieve its fullest potential. that may sound ambitious but its not, its the whole purpose of life to live our lives in our fullest potential in whatever we do. In the life of a product. its started with an idea then a project then a real product, if you are a founder you should be proud of yourself for taking that passing idea to come this far. but you just know that your product isnt in its fullest potential right now.

have you heard of the  phrase. "if you are growing bigger, you should ground yourself or else you will lose yourself in the process" and grounding yourself is easy while you are still growing . the same applies for a product too. 

### before you build more , define the product again. when  ?

If you have a real product, running for months ,with real users, real workflows and real engineers, there comes a feeling, everything is getting slower but nothing is obviously broken and then you will start to wonder that your child while keep on building this way will reach its fullest potential ? and your answer is 'i am not sure'  then it is the time !

### Nothing is broken, really ?
The problem isn't that something has broken. The problem is that you're starting to lose the ability to know what might break. this may be felt in small moments like Someone asks, “If we change this, what else does it affect?” And the room goes quiet. You can explain what two parts of the product do. But you can't quite explain why changing one quietly changes the other. A feature that should take two days takes two weeks. New engineers take longer to become useful. You ask the engineer who knows the system best what a change will affect. The answer is, “Let me check.” 

everyone of these problems is survivable and that's the frustrating part because nothing is obviously broken and product is still working, users are using it and the team is still shipping then why are you wondering about your child's fullest potential will be reached . why are you taking these instances as a warning ?

Its because underneath all of this, something has changed. Everyone can explain their own part. No one can quite explain how all of it fits together anymore. The system has grown, but the shared understanding of the system hasn't kept up. And that's the problem with growing same as adulting.
