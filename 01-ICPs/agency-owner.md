# Agency Owner ICP

## Why this ICP?

Prateek mentioned that agency owners are currently the most important audience for ColoredCow because that's where a significant part of the company's work and revenue comes from.

This document is an attempt to understand their decisions before extracting stories from the Canonicals.

---

## Who are they?

An agency owner delivering digital products or software for clients.

They already have client relationships they value, but need an engineering partner they can trust to help deliver the work without damaging those relationships.

---

## What are they deciding?

They're not only deciding whether ColoredCow can build software.

They're deciding:

- Can I trust them with my client?
- Will they think beyond execution?
- Will they help me solve problems I haven't noticed?
- Will they become part of my team rather than another vendor?
- Will they protect my relationship with my client?

---

## What evidence helps them decide?

Not claims.

Real stories.

Stories that show how ColoredCow:

- became part of a client's team
- helped clients make better decisions
- shared responsibility
- stayed long enough to understand the system
- created value beyond the original scope

---

## Current hypothesis

The website should answer these decisions through real client stories rather than explaining ColoredCow's philosophy.
